Who are we?

We are a group of committed parents and community members who are dedicated to the continued operation of Wind in the Willows while restoring the stability that was lost during the period of uncertainty. We established a not-for-profit incorporated association in May this year and intend to submit a proposal to the Town of Bassendean for the transfer of the service.

We have been working hard behind the scenes, preparing to become an approved daycare operator and learning from parent and community-led centres such as Little Possums, Marjorie Mann and other organisations that operate as community-managed not-for-profits. We have also been collaborating with peak agencies that support this model to ensure we are ready to create something exceptional for Bassendean families.

How would it work?

A parent and community-led committee would become legally responsible for the service upon transfer. The Willows Manager and Assistant Manager would continue to oversee the day-to-day running of the centre in accordance with existing policies and procedures and would attend meetings with the committee, so there would be minimal disruption to daily operations.

A parent and community-led committee would meet monthly with the Manager to oversee governance and operational matters. The committee would consider organisational issues, pass motions and vote on key decisions relating to the centre. It would ultimately be responsible for the overall operation of the service, including financial oversight, with the assistance and guidance of the Manager.

Our committee is currently made up of parents and community members with backgrounds in accounting, law, child health, governance, compliance and government. If the Town agrees to transfer the service to us, it is our intention to hold an Annual General Meeting in late 2026 or early 2027 so anyone with relevant skills and/or experience can also apply to become a committee member. Committee members would be appointed through an election process, and members of the organisation would be eligible to vote.

We would love you to become a member and help build the future of a community-led early childhood service for Bassendean.

Become involved

We welcome anyone who has skills or professional experience that may assist us, especially in governance, organisational management, specific childcare knowledge and fundraising.

If you would like to get involved, please contact us or visit the support page to learn how you can donate or become a member.
