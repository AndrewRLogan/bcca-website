Becoming a member

Any person who supports the objects or purposes of the Association is eligible to apply to become a member. The Bassendean Community Children’s Association Inc. is made up of office-holding committee members and ordinary committee members, alongside ordinary members who are able to vote at Annual General Meetings and elect committee members.

Any parent or community member can become a member by submitting an application and paying a $5 membership fee. If the proposal is not ultimately successful, the membership fee is refunded.

Please contact us to receive a membership application.

Ways to help

  • Become an ordinary member of the association for a $5 membership contribution.
  • Volunteer your time and expertise in governance, compliance, fundraising, or community engagement.
  • Spread the word and encourage other families and community members to support the proposal.
  • Attend meetings or get in touch if you can assist with skills, donations, or resources.
