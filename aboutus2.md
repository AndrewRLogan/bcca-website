What is the Bassendean Community Children’s Association Inc?

We are a group of committed parents and community members who are dedicated to the continued operation of Wind in the Willows but reverting back to how things were before all of this uncertainty arose. We established a Not-for-Profit Incorporated Association in May this year and intend to submit a proposal to the Town of Bassendean, for the transfer of Wind in the Willows. We have been working hard behind the scenes, getting prepared to become an approved daycare operator and learning lots from our friends at Little Possums, Marjorie Mann and other centres that operate as parent and community-led not-for profits, just like we hope to! We have also been collaborating with peak agencies that support this type of daycare model to learn everything we need to know to make this a success, and we feel confident that, if successful, we are going to create something amazing for Bassendean families.


We formed an official committee to run the Association and six committee members have been appointed – see more about us below. Many of the committee members are current Willows parents and we welcome you to approach us at daycare to chat about any of this.

HOW WOULD A PARENT AND COMMUNITY-LED NOT FOR PROFIT CHILDCARE WORK?

A parent and community-led committee would become legally responsible for the service upon transfer. The Willows Manager and Assistant Manager (Nicole and Kirstie) would continue to oversee the day-to-day running of the centre in accordance with the existing policies and procedures and attend meetings with the committee. There would be minimal impact on the daily operations of the service. 
 

A parent and community-led committee would meet monthly with the Manager to oversee governance and operational matters. The committee would consider organisational issues, pass motions and vote on key decisions relating to the centre. The committee would ultimately be responsible for the overall operation of the service, including financial oversight, with the assistance and guidance of the Manager. The organisation would operate in a similar way to other parent and community-led centres such as Meela Child Care Centre, Little Possums, Brockman House, Marjorie Mann and Rainbow Child Care.

 

WHO IS ON THE COMMITTEE?

Our community is currently made up of parents of the service and community members with backgrounds in areas such as accounting, law, child health, government, governance and compliance. If the Town agrees to transfer the service to us,  it is our intention to hold an Annual General Meeting in late 2026 or early 2027, so that anyone else who may have relevant skills and/or experience may also apply to become a committee member. Committee members would be appointed through an election process with members of the organisation eligible to vote. We would love you to become a member!

POTENTIAL BENEFITS OF A COMMUNITY-LED COMMITTEE

A parent and community-led volunteer committee may offer several advantages:

Willows would remain locally owned and supported by the community it serves.
From the perspective of children and educators, the centre would operate similarly to how it does now. Only the governance and administrative structure would change.
There would be opportunities for community fundraising initiatives to support equipment upgrades, educational resources and facility improvements.
Lower fees could be offered, as committee members would be volunteers and funds could be directed toward staff, resources and service improvements.
Our community includes many capable professionals who could contribute valuable expertise. The strong local connection to Willows may also help ensure ongoing commitment and engagement.
