Bassendean Community Children’s Association

We are a not-for-profit community association seeking to take over the operation of Wind in the Willows through the Expression of Interest currently open with the Town of Bassendean.

Who we are

Bassendean Community Children’s Association Inc. (BCCA) is a not-for-profit incorporated association made up of local parents and community members. We have no shareholders and no profit motive — every dollar goes back into the centre, the children and the educators.

What we’re doing

The Town of Bassendean has opened an Expression of Interest for the operation of the Wind in the Willows childcare centre. BCCA has been formed to submit an EOI so that the centre stays in community hands, run for the benefit of local families rather than for profit.

Get involved

We’d love to hear from families, educators and community members who want to support the centre.
